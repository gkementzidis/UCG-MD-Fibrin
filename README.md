# Investigating Fibrin Assembly with Coarse-Grained Molecular Dynamics: Effect of αC Regions

The **three folders** include files from the three types of simulations we ran throughout the paper:
1. **GROMACS**: all-atom (AA) molecular dynamics (MD) simulations of fibrin with explicit solvent (TIP3P) at 310K, 1 bar.
2. **NAMD**: coarse-grained (CG) MD of a fibrin monomer under Langevin dynamics; conducted during the iterations of Iterative Boltzmann Inversion Method (IBIM).
3. **LAMMPS**: CG MD of multiple fibrin monomers in a large 600nm x 300nm x 300nm box with the parametrized force field under Langevin dynamics.
